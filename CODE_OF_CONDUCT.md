# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our standards

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome newcomers, assume good faith, accept
feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project and its users.
Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory or
sexualised language, publishing others' private information, and other conduct
that would reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
other official channel — and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer via GitHub. Reports
will be reviewed and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit, or
reject contributions, and may warn or ban contributors, for behaviour that
violates this code.
